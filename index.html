Executive summary
Instant forms are building momentum, with the latest week the strongest yet.

Since launching on Aug 25, the Emface campaign has invested $2,048, reaching 8,054 people with 25.8K impressions. Instant forms went live on Sep 7 and have generated 45 leads. Measured against total spend, that is $45.52 per lead.

Facebook carried 75.8% of spend and delivered 39 of 45 leads. Instagram took 24.2% and added 6. The last 7 days produced 17 leads at about $20.60 each on steady spend. Frequency is now 3.20, so fresh creative in the coming weeks will help keep that pace.
